Rebates

What you owe each referrer for the month: their percentage of every referred patient's full bill, rebuilt registration by registration from the tracker.

Payouts are the referrer's rate × what each patient actually paid: price-list gross plus any per-test surcharge, less any MD discount recorded as a DISC:n% tag in the comments — never read from the AMOUNT column, which records the payment net of the rebate.
